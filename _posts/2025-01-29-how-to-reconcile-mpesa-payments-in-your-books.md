M-Pesa moves faster than your bookkeeping. By the time you sit down at the end of the month to reconcile, you have hundreds of transactions across dozens of customers, and no easy way to match them to invoices.

The businesses that stay on top of it all follow the same four-step process.

## 1. Record the transaction code, not just "M-Pesa"

The moment a customer pays, record the ten-character M-Pesa confirmation code against the invoice. Not "M-Pesa" as a method. The actual code.

That code is your receipt. If a customer ever disputes a payment, or if KRA asks, the code proves or disproves it in seconds.

Some businesses take a screenshot of the customer's confirmation SMS and attach it to the invoice. That works too, but the code is faster to search.

## 2. Separate business and personal M-Pesa

The most dangerous habit is accepting customer payments into a personal M-Pesa line. It seems harmless. It is not.

Three problems:

1. You cannot prove the payment in a dispute
2. It never makes it into your business books
3. KRA treats it as personal income, not business revenue

If you take payments personally, transfer everything to the business M-Pesa same day and record it against the invoice.

## 3. Download your Safaricom statement

At month end, log in to the M-Pesa portal and download your business line's statement. It arrives as a CSV with every transaction: timestamp, code, amount, and counterparty.

Match each line against a recorded payment in your books. Anything unmatched is either:

- A payment you recorded in the system but never actually received (rare)
- A payment you received but never recorded (common)

Either way, the reconciliation reveals it.

## 4. Investigate discrepancies the same day

Do not batch discrepancies. If the statement shows KES 45,000 in and your books show KES 42,000, investigate while the transactions are fresh. Ask the customer, look at the code, check the invoicing.

At the end of the month, when you have 40 discrepancies, you will not have the patience to chase any of them.

## The tools that help

If you use accounting software that supports M-Pesa reconciliation, you can usually import the Safaricom CSV and match it to recorded payments in bulk. That turns a day of work into an hour.

If you are doing this manually in Excel, you can still make it work. But set aside a fixed hour every Friday for reconciliation, and do not let it pile up.

## Why it matters

A clean reconciliation gives you three things:

- **Accurate cash flow.** You know exactly how much came in and when.
- **Audit readiness.** If KRA asks, you can produce supporting evidence for every transaction.
- **Customer confidence.** When a customer claims they paid and you can confirm or deny in seconds, you look professional.

The alternative is a shoebox of screenshots and a permanent sense of uncertainty.
