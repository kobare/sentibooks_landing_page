Spreadsheets are how most Kenyan businesses start. They are free, flexible, and familiar. But there comes a point where they cost more than they save.

Here are the seven signs you have reached it.

## 1. Month-end takes more than a day

If closing the month takes 3 days, 5 days, or a week, the spreadsheet is the bottleneck. Real accounting software closes a month in an hour.

## 2. You cannot answer "who owes us what?" without digging

If you cannot open a screen and see aged receivables by customer, you do not actually know where your cash is stuck. Spreadsheets can track this, but rarely get maintained well enough to be reliable.

## 3. VAT returns are a scramble

If preparing the VAT3 return requires manually re-tallying a month of transactions, you are one miscount away from a penalty. Software calculates output VAT and input VAT automatically and exports the return.

## 4. You have employees

Payroll in a spreadsheet works for 1 or 2 employees. Past 3, the statutory deduction calculations, payslip generation, and monthly filings become error-prone. One PAYE mistake costs more than a year of accounting software.

## 5. You cannot produce a balance sheet

The balance sheet is not optional. Banks want it when you apply for credit. Investors want it. KRA uses it for assessment. If you cannot produce a reliable one in 10 minutes, you are flying blind.

## 6. Inventory is managed by guesswork

If you are not sure what is in stock without walking the storeroom, your inventory is being managed by chance. Software tracks SKUs, movement history, and reorder points automatically.

## 7. You are losing receipts

If your purchase records consist of a WhatsApp album, a drawer, and a folder of papers, some expenses will never make it into your books. Every missing receipt is either untracked profit or a lost tax deduction.

## The cost of not switching

A business with KES 10 million annual revenue that loses 3 percent of expenses to poor tracking loses KES 300,000 a year. On top of that:

- Missed VAT deductions
- Late filing penalties
- Time wasted on manual reconciliation
- Decisions made on stale information

For most businesses, the ROI on accounting software is measured in weeks, not years.

## How to choose software

Match the software to how you actually work:

- **You invoice a lot.** Look for good invoicing and recurring billing.
- **You sell at a counter.** You need POS and thermal receipts.
- **You have inventory.** SKU tracking and stock movement are essential.
- **You have employees.** Payroll with statutory calculations is not optional.
- **You operate multiple branches.** Multi-branch or workspace support is essential.

Ignore features you will not use. A simpler tool you actually use beats a complex one you avoid.
