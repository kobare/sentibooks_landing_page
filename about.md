## Why we built Sentibooks

Running a business in Kenya means navigating a tax system most small business owners were never trained for. VAT returns. PAYE. SHIF. NSSF. Housing Levy. Annual returns. Every one has a deadline and a penalty if you miss it.

The accounting software that exists is either built for foreign markets with different tax systems, or it is spreadsheet-shaped and expects you to be an accountant. Neither fits the way Kenyan SMEs actually work.

Sentibooks is built for the Kenyan business owner who wants to invoice, track expenses, run payroll, and know where they stand at the end of the month, without having to become an accountant to do it.

## What we do

Sentibooks is a single platform that covers the full business cycle:

- **Invoicing and quotations** with automatic VAT calculation
- **Recurring invoices** for retainer and subscription customers
- **POS** for over-the-counter sales with thermal receipts
- **Work orders and tasks** for service businesses
- **Inventory management** with movement tracking and low-stock alerts
- **Purchases and expenses** with supplier bills and LPOs
- **Banking and reconciliation** for bank and M-Pesa transactions
- **Payroll** with PAYE, SHIF, NSSF, and Housing Levy calculated automatically
- **Full accounting**, including trial balance, balance sheet, general ledger, journal entries, and fixed asset register

Everything is built for Kenyan tax requirements out of the box. A pre-loaded chart of accounts. Automatic VAT on every transaction. KRA PIN stored against your organisation profile.
